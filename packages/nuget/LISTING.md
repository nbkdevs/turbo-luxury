**Short description:** Estimate how many litres a year car washing uses by method (hose, pressure washer, bucket, rinseless, waterless), compare methods and see the savings.

**Features:** litres per wash ranges for five methods; annual totals; washes per year from an interval; comparison with percent saved and bathtub equivalents; optional cost from your own tariff; targets netstandard2.0 and net8.0; no dependencies.

**Publish:** `dotnet pack src -c Release` then `dotnet nuget push` with the owner's API key (not run).
