# Mobile Car Wash for a Nissan Patrol in Al Muroor, Abu Dhabi

A black Nissan Patrol received a mobile exterior wash in Al Muroor, Abu Dhabi. The vehicle was cleaned at its location using a foam-wash process, with the finished photographs showing the difference after the exterior cleaning was completed.

## Client & Vehicle

**Client:** Hassan M.  

**Location:** Al Muroor, Abu Dhabi  

**Vehicle:** Nissan Patrol  

**Package:** Quick Shine  

**Service:** Mobile car wash

## The Situation

The Nissan Patrol had picked up everyday road dust and exterior dirt from regular driving around Abu Dhabi.

The owner wanted a routine exterior clean without taking the SUV to a fixed wash center. The vehicle was already parked at a suitable location in Al Muroor, making it a straightforward fit for mobile service.

The photographs from this appointment focus on the exterior washing process and the finished vehicle.

## The Cleaning Process

### Foam Wash

The Nissan Patrol was covered with cleaning foam across the exterior, including the rear body panels, windows, wheels and lower sections.

The foam stage was captured while the vehicle was still in the middle of the wash.

### Exterior Washing

The team worked across the SUV's visible exterior surfaces to remove the accumulated road dirt and surface contamination.

The vehicle remained in the same parking area throughout the service.

### Wheels and Lower Exterior

The wheels, lower bodywork and rear section were also part of the exterior cleaning shown in the job photographs.

These areas can collect road dust and dirt during everyday driving, so they form an important part of a complete exterior wash.

## Mobile Car Washing in an Urban Abu Dhabi Location

The photos show the practical side of the mobile setup clearly.

The Nissan Patrol remained parked at the customer's location while the wash was carried out around it.

There was no need to take the SUV to a fixed wash center. The cleaning was performed on location, with the vehicle ready to use once the service was finished.

## The Result

The difference between the two stages is clear.

The first photograph shows the Patrol covered in foam during the wash. The second shows the same vehicle after cleaning, with the black exterior visibly cleaner and the bodywork showing natural reflections again.

The surrounding location remains the same, making the before-and-after comparison straightforward.
