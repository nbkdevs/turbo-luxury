# Hot-climate car care

Instruction-only: no commands to run. Use these rules when answering, and adapt them to the user's actual weather if they give it.

## When to use

- "Is it okay to wash my car at noon in July?"
- "There was a dust storm yesterday. How do I clean the car without scratching it?"
- "How do I stop the dashboard cracking in the sun?"
- "Is it safe to leave my power bank in the car?"

## When to wash

- Avoid washing in direct sun when it is above about 38 C (100 F), or whenever the panels are too hot to keep a hand on. Hot panels dry soap and water into spots. Wash early in the morning or in the evening, or in full shade.
- Strong wind blows grit onto wet paint, so wait for it to drop below about 25 km/h.
- In humid weather water dries slowly and leaves marks: dry with a clean microfibre drying towel and do the glass by hand.

## Dust, sand and storms

- Rinse first with plenty of low-pressure water so loose grit floats off. Never wipe a dusty panel with a dry cloth or a duster.
- Use two buckets (one soapy, one rinse) or a fresh microfibre mitt per panel, and work from the roof down.
- After a storm, rinse the wipers, the windscreen seal and the radiator grille, and replace the cabin air filter more often in dusty months.

## Paint

- With outdoor parking, wax lasts about 1-2 months and a sealant 3-6 months. A ceramic coating lasts 1-2 years. Polish first if there are swirls, oxidation or etched water spots.
- Bird droppings, bug splatter and tree sap get worse in heat. Soften them with a wet cloth and lift them within a day.
- A breathable, soft-lined car cover helps with long-term parking but scratches if put over a dusty car.

## Interior

- Use a windscreen sunshade and ventilate the cabin for a minute (windows down, fan on) before driving.
- Wipe the dashboard and trim with a UV-protectant interior product, not a greasy silicone shine.
- Condition leather every 2-3 months in strong sun. Blot spills, never rub.
- Do not leave aerosols, lighters, power banks, phones, medicines or plastic water bottles in a parked car in summer. Never leave children or pets in it, even for a few minutes.

## Tyres, battery and cooling

- Check tyre pressure when the tyres are cold (before driving or in the morning). Heat raises pressure, and sun and heat crack older tyres faster: check the sidewalls and the date code.
- Heat shortens battery life. Test a battery older than about 2-3 years before summer.
- Keep coolant at the mark and the AC condenser free of dust and leaves.

## Notes
