# Paint protection chooser

Instruction-only: no commands, installs or network access. Use the table and rules below.

## When to use

- "Wax, sealant or ceramic for a car parked outside all day?"
- "I applied sealant on 1 September. When is it due again?"
- "There are swirl marks. Do I need to polish before protecting?"
- "Ceramic costs 1,500 here. What is that per year?"

## Products

Durability is a planning estimate for strong sun, heat and dust, which is shorter than many label claims.

| Product | Lasts (months) | Typical | UV resistance (1-5) | Look | Polish first? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Carnauba wax | 1-2 | 1.5 | 2 | warm, deep gloss | no |
| Paint sealant | 3-6 | 4.5 | 3 | sharp, reflective gloss | no |
| Ceramic coating | 12-24 | 18 | 5 | hard, glassy gloss | yes |

In a mild climate or garage parking, durability is closer to the top of each range.

## 1. Choose

Start every product at 0 points and add:

- Parking outdoor: ceramic +3, sealant +2. Covered: sealant +3, ceramic +1.5, wax +1. Garage: wax +2.5, sealant +2.
- Keeping the car 3+ years: ceramic +2. Under 18 months: wax +2, sealant +1.
- Tight or do-it-yourself budget: wax +2, ceramic -3.
- Paint not polished recently: ceramic -1 (and budget for polishing if ceramic still wins).

Recommend the highest score (ties: alphabetical). Show the ranking and one line per rule that applied.

## 2. Renewal dates

- "Harsh" means parked outdoors all day: use the short end of the range (wax 1, sealant 3, ceramic 12 months). Otherwise use the typical value rounded (wax 2, sealant 5, ceramic 18 months).
- Due date = application date + that many months. If the day does not exist in the target month, use the last day of that month.
- For a reminder list, keep adding the same period (for example sealant applied 2026-09-01, harsh, 2 reminders: 2026-12-01 and 2027-03-01).

## 3. Yearly cost

Applications per year = 12 / months (same harsh rule, typical value not rounded), to 2 decimals. Yearly cost = applications per year x the price the user pays per application, in their currency. Ask for the price. Do not invent one. Example: ceramic at 1,500 typical = 12 / 18 = 0.67 a year, about 1,000 a year.

## 4. Polish first?

- A fingernail catches in a scratch: polishing only softens it. Suggest a professional look at it, and polish the surrounding paint before protecting.
- Swirl marks, oxidation (dull, chalky paint) or etched water spots: polish first, then protect so the product bonds to clean paint.
- Otherwise: wash, decontaminate (clay or iron remover), then protect. No polishing needed yet.

## Notes

- Real durability depends on the product brand, how it was applied and washing habits (harsh soaps and automatic brushes shorten it).
- A coating does not make a car scratch-proof or remove the need to wash it.
