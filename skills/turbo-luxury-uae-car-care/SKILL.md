## Rules of thumb

**When to wash**
- Avoid washing in direct sun above about 38 C. Hot panels bake soap and water spots into the paint. Prefer before 9am or after 6pm, or wash in full shade.
- After a dust or sand storm, rinse first with plenty of water so loose grit floats off. Never wipe a dusty panel with a dry cloth.
- Humid weeks dry slowly: use a microfibre drying towel and dry glass by hand.

**Paint**
- Wax or sealant every few months in outdoor parking. Polish first if there are swirls, oxidation or etched water spots.
- Bird droppings and bug splatter are acidic: soften with a wet cloth and lift them within a day or two.

**Interior**
- Use a windscreen sunshade and ventilate the cabin for a minute before driving.
- Wipe the dashboard and trim with a UV-protectant interior product, not a greasy shine product.
- Condition leather a few times a year. Blot spills, never rub.
- Do not leave aerosols, lighters, power banks or water bottles in a parked car in summer.

**Tyres**
- Check pressure when the tyres are cold. Heat raises pressure and old tyres crack faster in the sun.
