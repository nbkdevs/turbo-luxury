<h2 class="kt-adv-heading584_bedfd9-2c wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading584_bedfd9-2c">Why Wheels and Rims Get Their Own Treatment</h2>
<!-- /wp:kadence/advancedheading --><!-- wp:kadence/image {"id":790,"uniqueID":"584_17bf55-38","borderRadius":[20,20,20,20],"showCaption":false,"className":"tl-visual-card"} -->

<!-- /wp:kadence/image --><!-- wp:kadence/advancedheading {"uniqueID":"584_ba8f0d-ce","color":"#C8C8C8","markBorder":"","markBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"tabletMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"mobileMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"htmlTag":"p"} -->
<p class="kt-adv-heading584_ba8f0d-ce wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading584_ba8f0d-ce">Brake dust is mostly iron, worn off the rotor and pad every time you brake. Left on a wheel, that iron reacts with moisture in the air and corrodes the finish, which is why wheels that go too long without cleaning end up pitted or dull even when the rest of the car still looks fine. Detailing sources consistently point to this same mechanism: the metal filings set off a slow corrosive reaction against the wheel&#039;s own finish, not just sitting on top of it like ordinary dirt.</p>
<!-- /wp:kadence/advancedheading --><!-- wp:kadence/advancedheading {"uniqueID":"584_7fc03a-4a","color":"#C8C8C8","markBorder":"","markBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"tabletMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"mobileMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"htmlTag":"p"} -->
<p class="kt-adv-heading584_7fc03a-4a wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading584_7fc03a-4a">That&#039;s why we treat wheels and rims as their own cleaning step instead of folding them into the general exterior wash.</p>
