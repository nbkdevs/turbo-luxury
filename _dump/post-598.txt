<h2 class="kt-adv-heading584_fcaa0e-62 wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading584_fcaa0e-62">Why Wax Matters More in Abu Dhabi&#039;s Heat</h2>
<!-- /wp:kadence/advancedheading --><!-- wp:kadence/advancedheading {"uniqueID":"584_b7191e-42","color":"#C8C8C8","markBorder":"","markBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"tabletMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"mobileMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"htmlTag":"p"} -->
<p class="kt-adv-heading584_b7191e-42 wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading584_b7191e-42">Sun exposure breaks down paint over time. UV rays cause oxidation, which shows up as fading and a chalky look, and the effect is faster on cars parked outdoors in strong, constant sun. A wax layer acts as a barrier between the paint and that UV exposure, and it also helps stop bird droppings, tree sap, and dust from baking onto the surface in the heat.</p>
