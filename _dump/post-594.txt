<h2 class="kt-adv-heading584_695b78-4c wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading584_695b78-4c">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<!-- /wp:kadence/advancedheading --><!-- wp:details {"className":"tl-faq-item"} -->
<details class="wp-block-details tl-faq-item"><summary>How much does a mobile car wash cost in Mohammed Bin Zayed City?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The same as anywhere in Abu Dhabi: 60–70 AED for Quick Shine, 100–120 AED for Golden Shine, 150–170 AED for Diamond Shine, and 300 AED for Detailing Shine.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></details>
<!-- /wp:details --><!-- wp:details {"className":"tl-faq-item"} -->
<details class="wp-block-details tl-faq-item"><summary>Do I need to provide water or electricity?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>No. Our team brings everything needed to complete the service.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></details>
<!-- /wp:details --><!-- wp:details {"className":"tl-faq-item"} -->
<details class="wp-block-details tl-faq-item"><summary>How often should I wash my car in MBZ City?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Weekly if it's parked outdoors, since dust builds up quickly on inland streets. Covered parking can stretch that to every 10 to 14 days.</p>
