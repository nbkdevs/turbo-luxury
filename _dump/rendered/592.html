In your area

Al Reem Island and Your Car

Al Reem Island is one of Abu Dhabi's most built-up waterfront communities, made up mostly of high-rise apartment towers rather than villas. It's popular with young professionals and families who want a marina-side, urban lifestyle close to the city center.

Being surrounded by water brings salt air and humidity into the mix, which speeds up the sun's dulling effect on a parked car's exterior. Combined with the density of towers, most residents rely on covered basement or podium parking, which we're used to working around.

Choosing a level

Which Package Fits Your Al Reem Island Home?

Quick Shine suits routine upkeep. Golden Shine adds the wax layer that matters most this close to the water. Diamond Shine brings in the engine bay and sanitization. Detailing Shine is the full package, through to AC sanitization and interior restoration.

Where we work

Can You Wash My Car in My Al Reem Island Building's Parking?

Yes, in most cases, once your building is on board. Since almost everyone here parks in shared basement or podium parking rather than a private driveway, it's worth a quick check with building security or management before booking, the same as for any service vehicle. Once that's confirmed, our team works around your building's access process.
