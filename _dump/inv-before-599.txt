h2	When Does Your Car Need Deep Interior Cleaning?
p	Consider it if dust has built up inside the cabin, the car is used daily, or it&#039;s been a long stretch since the last proper clean. Regular cleaning keeps a well-kept interior fresh. Deep cleaning is for catching up.
h2	Interior Cleaning vs Interior Sanitization
p	Cleaning removes dirt and debris from surfaces. Sanitization, included in Diamond Shine and Detailing Shine, goes a step further to treat the cabin itself. They&#039;re related but not the same service, which is why we list them separately.
h2	Does AC Sanitization Actually Make a Difference?
img	869 | tl-visual-card | Steam cleaning car AC vents during interior sanitization in Abu Dhabi | interior-ac-steam-sanitize.png
p	Yes, and there&#039;s real research behind it. A study published in BMC Infectious Diseases tested car cabins and found that running the AC system cut the total number of airborne microorganisms by 81.7%, mold spores by 83.3%, and particles by 87.8%, within minutes of switching it on.
p	The catch is the system itself. A separate peer-reviewed study found that AC filters in passenger cars often carry fungal contamination, with higher levels in summer than winter, which is exactly Abu Dhabi&#039;s climate for most of the year. Researchers in both studies recommended regular filter changes and system disinfection, not just running the AC and assuming it&#039;s clean. That&#039;s what our AC sanitization service, included in Detailing Shine, is built to address.
