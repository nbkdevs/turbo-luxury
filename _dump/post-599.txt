<!-- /wp:kadence/advancedheading --><!-- wp:kadence/advancedheading {"uniqueID":"584_751ec3-cd","color":"#C8C8C8","markBorder":"","markBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"tabletMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"mobileMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"htmlTag":"p"} -->
<p class="kt-adv-heading584_751ec3-cd wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading584_751ec3-cd">Cleaning removes dirt and debris from surfaces. <h2 class="kt-adv-heading584_bd5a4c-51 wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading584_bd5a4c-51">Does AC Sanitization Actually Make a Difference?</h2>
<!-- /wp:kadence/advancedheading --><!-- wp:kadence/image {"id":869,"uniqueID":"584_4c69f0-56","borderRadius":[20,20,20,20],"showCaption":false,"className":"tl-visual-card","url":"http://turbo-luxury-car-wash.local/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/interior-ac-steam-sanitize.png","alt":"Steam cleaning car AC vents during interior sanitization in Abu Dhabi"} -->

<!-- /wp:kadence/image --><!-- wp:kadence/advancedheading {"uniqueID":"584_dd59f2-3b","color":"#C8C8C8","markBorder":"","markBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"tabletMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"mobileMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"htmlTag":"p"} -->
<p class="kt-adv-heading584_dd59f2-3b wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading584_dd59f2-3b">Yes, and there&#039;s real research behind it. A study published in BMC Infectious Diseases tested car cabins and found that running the AC system cut the total number of airborne microorganisms by 81.7%, mold spores by 83.3%, and particles by 87.8%, within minutes of switching it on.</p>
<!-- /wp:kadence/advancedheading --><!-- wp:kadence/advancedheading {"uniqueID":"584_8349cc-3f","color":"#C8C8C8","markBorder":"","markBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"tabletMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"mobileMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"htmlTag":"p"} -->
<p class="kt-adv-heading584_8349cc-3f wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading584_8349cc-3f">The catch is the system itself. A separate peer-reviewed study found that AC filters in passenger cars often carry fungal contamination, with higher levels in summer than winter, which is exactly Abu Dhabi&#039;s climate for most of the year. Researchers in both studies recommended regular filter changes and system disinfection, not just running the AC and assuming it&#039;s clean. That&#039;s what our AC sanitization service, included in Detailing Shine, is built to address.</p>
