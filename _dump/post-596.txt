<p>The same as anywhere in Abu Dhabi: 60–70 AED for Quick Shine, 100–120 AED for Golden Shine, 150–170 AED for Diamond Shine, and 300 AED for Detailing Shine.</p>

<details class="wp-block-details tl-faq-item"><summary>How often should I wash my car in Shakhbout City?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Weekly is a good baseline if the car is parked outdoors, given how much dust settles on inland plots. Covered parking can stretch that to every 10 to 14 days.</p>
