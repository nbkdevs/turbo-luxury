<h2 class="kt-adv-heading53_17e441-9d wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading53_17e441-9d">How Often Should You Wash Your Car in <em class="tl-gold">Abu Dhabi?</em></h2>
<!-- /wp:kadence/advancedheading -->

<!-- wp:kadence/advancedheading {"uniqueID":"53_180b36-9e","color":"#C8C8C8","markBorder":"","markBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"tabletMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"mobileMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"htmlTag":"p"} -->
<p class="kt-adv-heading53_180b36-9e wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading53_180b36-9e">If your car is parked outdoors or driven daily, wash it weekly. Dust and sand settle fast in this climate. Cars kept in covered or garage parking can usually stretch to every 10 to 14 days.</p>
<!-- /wp:kadence/advancedheading -->

<!-- wp:kadence/advancedheading {"uniqueID":"53_18322b-9f","color":"#C8C8C8","markBorder":"","markBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"tabletMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"mobileMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"htmlTag":"p"} -->
<p class="kt-adv-heading53_18322b-9f wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading53_18322b-9f">Ordinary dust is mostly a look-and-feel issue. Bird droppings, tree sap, and insect residue are different. Left on the paint too long, they can etch the finish. Clean those off promptly, on any schedule.</p>
<!-- /wp:kadence/advancedheading -->

<h2 class="kt-adv-heading53_18f4f4-a4 wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading53_18f4f4-a4">Why a Clean Car Is About More Than <em class="tl-gold">Looks</em></h2>
<!-- /wp:kadence/advancedheading -->

<!-- wp:kadence/advancedheading {"uniqueID":"53_191be9-a5","color":"#C8C8C8","markBorder":"","markBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"tabletMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"mobileMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"htmlTag":"p"} -->
<p class="kt-adv-heading53_191be9-a5 wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading53_191be9-a5">A dirty interior isn't just untidy. Researchers at Aston University swabbed used cars for bacteria and found the car boot carried around 1,425 bacteria per sample, and the driver's seat around 649, both higher than typical readings from a household toilet. The dashboard, gearstick, and back seat also came in above toilet-level counts. Interiors that go longer between cleans build up more of this over time.</p>
<!-- /wp:kadence/advancedheading -->

<!-- wp:kadence/advancedheading {"uniqueID":"53_1942de-a6","color":"#C8C8C8","markBorder":"","markBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"tabletMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"mobileMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"htmlTag":"p"} -->
<p class="kt-adv-heading53_1942de-a6 wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading53_1942de-a6">None of this means a car will make you sick. It means the inside of a car collects more than dust, especially if food, gym bags, or shopping sit in it regularly. Regular interior cleaning, including sanitization on our Diamond Shine and Detailing Shine packages, keeps that buildup from setting in.</p>
<!-- /wp:kadence/advancedheading -->

<!-- wp:kadence/advancedheading {"uniqueID":"53_1969d3-a7","color":"#C8C8C8","markBorder":"","markBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"tabletMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"mobileMarkBorderStyles":[{"top":[null,"",""],"right":[null,"",""],"bottom":[null,"",""],"left":[null,"",""],"unit":"px"}],"htmlTag":"p"} -->
<p class="kt-adv-heading53_1969d3-a7 wp-block-kadence-advancedheading" data-kb-block="kb-adv-heading53_1969d3-a7">On the exterior side, a car's condition and appearance are well known to affect what buyers and dealers offer for it. We don't have one verified figure that isolates cleanliness alone, since resale studies usually bundle it with dents, scratches, and mechanical condition. What's consistent across that research is the direction: a car that looks cared for tends to be valued higher than one that looks neglected, all else being equal.</p>

<p>Turbo Luxury's packages run from 60 to 300 AED. Quick Shine is 60–70 AED, Golden Shine 100–120 AED, Diamond Shine 150–170 AED, and Detailing Shine 300 AED.</p>
