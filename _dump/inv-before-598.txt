h2	Car Wash vs Car Detailing
p	A car wash is a quick clean. Detailing goes further, with deep interior work, sanitization, engine bay cleaning, and finishing touches like front light polishing. Our Diamond Shine and Detailing Shine packages sit in that detailing range. Quick Shine and Golden Shine stay closer to a thorough regular wash.
h2	Why Wax Matters More in Abu Dhabi&#039;s Heat
p	Sun exposure breaks down paint over time. UV rays cause oxidation, which shows up as fading and a chalky look, and the effect is faster on cars parked outdoors in strong, constant sun. A wax layer acts as a barrier between the paint and that UV exposure, and it also helps stop bird droppings, tree sap, and dust from baking onto the surface in the heat.
p	This is why wax application starts at our Golden Shine and carries through Diamond Shine and Detailing Shine. In a climate like Abu Dhabi&#039;s, it&#039;s less of an upgrade and more of a basic layer of protection.
