<details class="wp-block-details tl-faq-item"><summary>How much does a mobile car wash cost on Saadiyat Island?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The same as anywhere in Abu Dhabi: 60–70 AED for Quick Shine, 100–120 AED for Golden Shine, 150–170 AED for Diamond Shine, and 300 AED for Detailing Shine.</p>

<details class="wp-block-details tl-faq-item"><summary>Do I need to provide water or electricity?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->

<p>Yes. We use pH-balanced products and hand-cleaning methods, which suit new deliveries and ceramic coatings well, common on Saadiyat given the number of newer villas and apartments.</p>
