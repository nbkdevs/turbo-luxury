# Engine Compartment Cleaning for a MINI in Mohammed Bin Zayed City, Abu Dhabi

A MINI received engine compartment cleaning as part of a mobile car-cleaning service in Mohammed Bin Zayed City, Abu Dhabi. The before-and-after photographs show the difference between the dusty engine compartment and the cleaned finish.

**Client:** Daniel H.  
**Location:** Mohammed Bin Zayed City, Abu Dhabi  
**Vehicle:** MINI  
**Package:** Diamond Shine  
**Service:** Engine compartment cleaning

## The Situation

The vehicle's engine compartment had accumulated a visible layer of dust and everyday road contamination.

While the exterior of a car is cleaned regularly, the engine compartment can be overlooked. In this case, the customer wanted that area cleaned as part of a more comprehensive vehicle wash.

Because the car was already at home in Mohammed Bin Zayed City, the service was arranged as a mobile appointment.

## What We Did

### Engine Compartment Cleaning

The team worked through the accessible engine-compartment surfaces, removing the accumulated dust and surface contamination.

The focus was on cleaning the visible components and surrounding areas while working around the existing engine hardware.

The process was carried out at the customer's location as part of the mobile Diamond Shine service.

## The Result

The difference is clear between the two photographs.

Before cleaning, the engine compartment had a dusty, dull appearance across the engine cover and surrounding surfaces.

After cleaning, the black engine components and surrounding areas have a noticeably cleaner appearance, with the lettering and component details much easier to see.
