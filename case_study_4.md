# Mobile Car Wash for a Land Rover Defender in Khalifa City, Abu Dhabi

A Land Rover Defender received a mobile exterior wash at a residential location in Khalifa City, Abu Dhabi. The vehicle was cleaned on site, with the photographs showing the Defender during the foam-wash stage and after the exterior cleaning was completed.

## Client & Vehicle

**Client:** Faisal K.  

**Location:** Khalifa City, Abu Dhabi  

**Vehicle:** Land Rover Defender  

**Package:** Quick Shine  

**Service:** Mobile car wash

## The Situation

The Defender had picked up everyday road dust and exterior dirt from regular driving around Abu Dhabi.

The owner wanted a routine exterior clean without taking the SUV to a fixed wash center. With the vehicle already parked at home, a mobile wash meant the cleaning could be completed at the same location.

## The Cleaning Process

### Foam Wash

The Defender was covered with cleaning foam across the exterior, including the bonnet, windows, doors, wheels and lower bodywork.

The foam stage helped loosen the surface dirt before the exterior was rinsed and cleaned.

### Exterior Washing

The team worked around the Defender's exterior, cleaning the main body panels, windows and lower exterior areas.

The vehicle remained parked in the residential area throughout the service.

### Wheels and Lower Body

The wheels and lower exterior sections were also part of the cleaning.

These areas collect dust, sand and road grime during everyday driving, making them important parts of a routine exterior wash.

## Mobile Car Washing at a Khalifa City Home

The photographs show the practical side of the mobile service.

The Defender remained at the customer's residential location while the wash was performed around it.

There was no separate trip to a fixed wash center. The mobile service came directly to the vehicle, allowing the customer to have the selected wash completed while the car remained at home.
