# Deep Interior Cleaning for a Nissan Patrol in Al Bateen, Abu Dhabi

A Nissan Patrol received a deep interior cleaning at a residential location in Al Bateen, Abu Dhabi. The service focused on the cabin, including the seats, floor areas, dashboard, centre console and other accessible interior surfaces.

The Nissan Patrol needed more than a routine interior clean.
